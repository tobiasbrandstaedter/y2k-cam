# Look-Familie: 35 mm compact, disposable and expired film

Presets: Faded Film, 90s Summer, Expired Film, Disposable Camera, Warm Nostalgia, Cool Vintage, Pink Film, Beach Memories, Analog Summer 2003. Bausteine BS-2, BS-3, BS-6, BS-7, BS-12.

## What the real film did, in order

1. **Lens.** Cheap single element plastic lens on disposables: soft corners, barrel distortion, vignette. Effects: Vintage Lens Softness, Edge Distortion, Vignette, Soft Edge Darkening.
2. **Flash.** Built in flash close to the lens: bright subject, fast falloff, dark background. Effect: White Flash.
3. **Stray light.** Light leaks through the back or the film door burn in from the frame edge: hottest at the edge, uneven along it, streaky in the direction of film travel, yellow white at the core and orange to deep red at the fringe. Effect: Light Leak. Halation: light passes the emulsion, reflects off the film base and comes back as a red glow just outside highlights. Effect: Halation, now fed by a bright pass that thresholds before blurring, so only real highlights glow. Lens Flare: a veil, faint aperture rays and ghosts with color fringed rims along the line through the image center.
4. **Grain.** Silver halide crystals in clumps. On a print, the thin parts of the negative (shadows, midtones) show the most and the coarsest grain, clipped highlights almost none. Effect: Filmkorn: three octaves on rotated grids, a domain warp for irregular shapes, a slow clump field, size and strength following the exposure.
5. **Ageing.** Expired film loses contrast and drifts in color, often toward green or olive. Old rolls collect dust that prints as white specks, and fine scratches along the film path. Effects: Vintage Color Cast Olive Film, Staub & Kratzer.
6. **Print.** Lifted blacks, rolled off highlights, the lab's color cast. Effects: Fade, Schwarz anheben, Lichter abrunden, Split Toning.

## Preset notes

- Expired Film now uses the Olive Film cast and light dust and scratches; its light leak is a little weaker because the new leak shape covers more area.
- Disposable Camera gains a slight barrel distortion from the plastic lens. Flash unchanged, it had already been reduced for skin tones.
- No preset uses Lens Flare, Gaussian Blur, Motion Blur, Lens Blur or the Sepia Light cast. Each looks reasonable at its defaults, but none of them belongs to a preset's device: a flare needs a light source at the right spot in the photo, and the blur effects are tools rather than device traits.
